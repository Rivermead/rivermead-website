What the rate covers

Consultancy Services

01

Defence of Unmeritorious Claims

For lenders, energy suppliers, brokers and their insurers. We review claims early, identify those with no legal or factual basis, and set out a reasoned analysis that stops them. More than 5,500 claims reviewed for defendants.

02

Expert Witness & Forensic Analysis

Court-ready reports under CPR Part 35. Forensic reconstruction of accounts, commissions and charges, quantum of loss, joint statements with the other side's expert, and evidence at trial.

03

Case Law & Regulatory Analysis

In-depth analysis of the authorities that decide commission, disclosure and unfair-relationship claims, drawn from our own library of more than 70,000 full-text UK judgments and 4,000 Financial Ombudsman decisions, and set into the framework your case needs.

04

Tax & Structuring Consultancy

Tax consultancy and corporate structuring for businesses and individuals, including group and holding-company structures, reviewed against the rules and the case law that apply to them.

05

Regulatory Consultancy

FCA CONC, DISP and Consumer Duty, Financial Ombudsman Service complaints and FSCS claims. Advice for firms on their exposure, and for representatives on the strength of a complaint.

06

Strategy for Solicitors & Counsel

Case theory, merits and quantum review before issue, limitation, disclosure requests, Part 36 and settlement strategy, working directly with the instructing solicitor and counsel.

Artificial Intelligence in Your Business

AI can transform how a business works, or expose it to risks it has not priced in. Our advice comes from practice: we design and run AI research and document-analysis systems in our own work, built on a library of more than 70,000 UK judgments, and we follow closely the leading thinking on AI in the UK and internationally.

Where it fits

Terms

Fees are payable whatever the outcome of the matter. We do not work on a contingency, success-fee or percentage basis. Where we are instructed as an expert witness, our overriding duty is to the court under CPR Part 35, and we do not act as an adviser to either party on the same matter.

Expert reports and volume work for panel firms can be quoted as a fixed fee on request. Travel, court attendance and third-party disbursements are charged in addition.

The Rivermead Partnership Ltd is not a firm of solicitors and does not carry out reserved legal activities. Where a matter needs a solicitor, barrister or other regulated adviser, we work alongside them.
